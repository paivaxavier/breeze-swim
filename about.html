About Breeze Swim

Teaching confidence, one swimmer at a time

Breeze Swim was founded on a simple belief: every person deserves to feel safe in the water — regardless of age, ability, or where they're starting from.

Based in Flagler County, we specialize in aquatic survival skills and stroke fundamentals for swimmers as young as six months old. Lessons are private, patient, and built around each swimmer's individual pace.

Our approach

Patient & encouraging

No pressure, no rushing. Swimmers build trust at their own speed in a supportive environment.

Safety first

Survival skills come before strokes. We teach swimmers how to stay safe before we teach them how to swim fast.

All abilities

Every lesson is adapted to the swimmer in front of us — including those with anxiety, sensory needs, or physical differences.

Let's talk about your swimmer

We'd love to hear about your goals and answer any questions before your first lesson.
