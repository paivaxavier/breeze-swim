Meet your instructor

Brielle Goldberg

Founder & Swim Instructor, Breeze Swim

Brielle grew up watching her mom teach aquatic survival to babies as young as six months — and that early exposure shaped everything she does today. She is Academy of Aquatic Survival Training certified and holds Level 4 Adaptive Swim Whispers certification for swimmers who need a gentler, individualized approach.

Her lessons focus on aquatic survival skills — rolling to float, breath control, and swimming to safety.

She founded Breeze Swim to bring private, one-on-one lessons to families in Flagler County, and serves as Executive Director of WaterSafe Inc., a local nonprofit that expands access to swim lessons and water-safety education.
