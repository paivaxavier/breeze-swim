FAQ

Common questions

Answers for parents and swimmers considering lessons with Breeze Swim.

What age can my child start swim lessons?
Breeze Swim accepts swimmers as young as 6 months old. Infant and toddler lessons focus on water acclimation and basic survival skills with caregiver support. There is no upper age limit — adults are welcome too.
Do you work with swimmers of all abilities?
Yes. All abilities are welcome. Whether your swimmer is nervous around water, has sensory needs, or learns at a different pace — lessons are adapted with patience and a personalized plan.
What's the difference between survival skills and stroke lessons?
Aquatic survival focuses on water safety — floating, breath control, and self-rescue. Stroke fundamentals build on that foundation with proper freestyle, backstroke, and other technique. Many swimmers start with survival and progress to strokes when ready.
Are lessons private or in a group?
Lessons are one-on-one so every minute is focused on your swimmer. This allows us to move at the right pace and give full attention to safety and technique.
Where are lessons held?
Breeze Swim serves Flagler County, Florida. Lesson location details (pool address, etc.) are shared when you book.
Does a parent need to be in the water?
For infants and young toddlers, a parent or caregiver is typically in the water alongside the instructor. As swimmers grow more independent, they may transition to lessons without a parent in the pool — always at a pace that feels safe for everyone.
What should my swimmer bring?
A well-fitting swimsuit, towel, and sunscreen. Goggles are optional for beginners but helpful for older swimmers. For infants, a reusable swim diaper is required. We'll confirm anything else when you book.
How do I book a lesson?
Visit our contact page to reach out. Share your swimmer's age, experience level, and goals — we'll get back to you with availability and next steps.
