FAQ

Common questions

Answers for parents and swimmers considering lessons with Breeze Swim.

Getting started

What age can my child start swim lessons?
Breeze Swim accepts swimmers as young as 6 months old. Infant and toddler lessons focus on water acclimation and basic survival skills with caregiver support. There is no upper age limit — adults are welcome too.
How do I join the waitlist?
Visit our contact page and fill out the waitlist form. You can also email breezeswimlessons@gmail.com or message @breezeswimlessons with questions. Include your swimmer's age and experience level — Brielle will reach out when a spot opens.
Why is there a waitlist?
Lessons are one-on-one, 15 minutes, and four times per week. Most students complete a 4–6 week program before moving on, so spots stay filled for several weeks at a time. Joining the waitlist puts you in line for the next opening — Brielle contacts families in order as spots become available.
How long is a typical program?
Most students complete a 4–6 week program of private lessons — 15 minutes each, four times per week (Monday–Thursday). Length varies by age, goals, and how quickly skills click. Spots stay filled for the length of each family's program.
When are lessons offered?
Lessons run Monday through Thursday, four times per week. Each session is 15 minutes of one-on-one instruction. Afternoon lessons are offered March through October; morning options may be available in summer. New spots depend on the waitlist — join via the waitlist form or message @breezeswimlessons on Instagram for updates.

Pricing & programs

How much do lessons cost?
Lessons are $90 per week — four 15-minute sessions, Monday through Thursday ($22.50 per lesson). Most students complete a 4–6 week program. See full details on our lessons page.
Is there a registration fee?
Yes. There is a $50 annual registration fee, charged once per calendar year per swimmer.
Do you offer refresher lessons for former students?
Yes. Brielle offers refresher lessons for swimmers who have already completed a program — so they can maintain survival skills between seasons or after time away from the water. Contact breezeswimlessons@gmail.com directly to schedule. Same lesson format and rates apply.
Can lessons be held at my private pool?
Lessons are typically held at Brielle's private pool in Bunnell. In some cases, lessons can be held at your private pool — an additional fee applies. Email breezeswimlessons@gmail.com to ask about availability and pricing for your location.

About lessons

Do you work with swimmers of all abilities?
Yes. All abilities are welcome. Brielle is Level 4 Adaptive Swim Whispers certified, so whether your swimmer is nervous around water, has sensory needs, or learns at a different pace — lessons are adapted with patience and a personalized plan.
Are lessons private or in a group?
Lessons are one-on-one — 15 minutes of focused instruction, four times per week. Every minute is dedicated to your swimmer so Brielle can move at the right pace and give full attention to safety and skill-building.
Does a parent need to be in the water?
For infants and young toddlers, a parent or caregiver is typically in the water alongside the instructor. As swimmers grow more independent, they may transition to lessons without a parent in the pool — always at a pace that feels safe for everyone.

Location & logistics

Where are lessons held?
Lessons are typically held at Brielle's private pool in Bunnell, a city in Flagler County, FL. The exact address is shared when you're scheduled. Lessons at your private pool may be available for an additional fee — see pricing or email Brielle for details.
What should my swimmer bring?
A well-fitting swimsuit, towel, and sunscreen. Goggles are optional for beginners but helpful for older swimmers. For infants, a reusable swim diaper is required. We'll confirm anything else when you're scheduled.

We're happy to help — no question is too small.
