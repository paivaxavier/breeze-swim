Ages 6 months+

Infant & Toddler Aquatic Survival

Gentle, developmentally appropriate introduction to the water. Focus on water acclimation, breath control, floating, and basic self-rescue skills — always with patience and caregiver involvement when appropriate.

  • Water comfort & acclimation
  • Breath control & submersion readiness
  • Back float & survival floating
  • Safe entry & exit from water
All ages

Aquatic Survival Skills

Practical water safety skills that could save a life. Swimmers learn how to stay calm, float, and reach safety — building real confidence, not just comfort in the shallow end.

  • Treading water & floating
  • Self-rescue techniques
  • Water safety awareness
  • Panic management in water
Beginner to advanced

Stroke Fundamentals

Once a swimmer is water-safe, we build proper technique across the core strokes — starting with body position and breathing, then progressing at a pace that feels right.

  • Freestyle & backstroke basics
  • Breathing rhythm & timing
  • Kicking & body alignment
  • Breaststroke & butterfly intro
Inclusive

All Abilities Welcome

Every swimmer learns differently. Whether your child has anxiety around water, sensory needs, or a physical difference — lessons are adapted with patience, encouragement, and a plan built around their goals.

Not sure which program fits?

Reach out with your swimmer's age and experience level — we'll recommend the right starting point.
